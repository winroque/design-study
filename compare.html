Mantine versus Chakra

Mantine decides more for you and ships more ready-made pieces, which speeds up an internal product. Chakra decides less and is a better starting point for a design system with its own identity. Both are accessible and have dark mode.

Mantine versus Carbon

Both serve work screens well. Carbon is stricter and denser, with square corners and written content rules; Mantine is more flexible and easier to customize. Compare them in the labs Mantine e Carbon.

Watch out for

Mantine 9 requires React 19.2 and depends on one main maintainer. Chakra 3 broke a lot in the migration from version 2. Carbon’s look is strongly tied to IBM, with little room for a personality of your own.

Decision

Five questions before choosing

Platform

Where does the product run?

In a native app, the platform’s system beats any personal taste. Users already know how to use those components.

Pace

How many decisions per minute?

A few call for comfort and space. Many call for density and zero ornament, because every decorative pixel becomes repeated friction.

User

Beginner or expert?

Beginners need obvious cues for what is clickable. Experts want shortcuts, tables and fewer clicks.

Obligation

Is accessibility required?

Public services, healthcare and large companies require contrast and keyboard navigation. That rules out neumorphism and glass behind text.

Lifespan

How long does it need to last?

Fashionable styles date in two or three years. Neutral systems and the Swiss style age well.

What to choose in each case

Whatever the style

Rules that apply to all

Color with a job

Save strong color for the main action and for states (success, warning, error). If everything is colorful, nothing stands out.

Minimum contrast 4.5:1

The WCAG minimum for body text. Glass, neumorphism and light gray on white often fail here.

Touch targets of 44 to 48 px

Apple recommends 44 pt and Google 48 dp. That is why mobile systems have big corners and tall buttons.

Consistency beats style

A mediocre system applied rigorously works better than a beautiful style applied halfway.
